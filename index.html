Active Recall
1.2× EXP
Test yourself without looking at notes. The most evidence-backed method for long-term retention.
  • Close your notes completely
  • Write or say everything you know about the topic
  • Check notes and mark what you missed
  • Repeat on missed points only
Pro tip: Use question cards — write the question on front, answer on back. Never look at the answer first.
📆
Spaced Repetition
Optimal Retention
Review material at increasing intervals to exploit the spacing effect.
  • Study topic for the first time today
  • Review after 1 day
  • Review after 3 days
  • Review after 1 week, then 2 weeks
Pro tip: Use Anki or a paper box system. Hard cards come back sooner, easy cards later.
✍️
Brain Dump / Blurt
Fast & Effective
Write everything you know on a blank page. Simple, fast, and brutal self-assessment.
  • Get a blank piece of paper
  • Set 10 minutes — write EVERYTHING you know
  • Compare with your notes in red pen
  • Re-blurt just the red items tomorrow
Pro tip: Don't re-read before blurting — that defeats the purpose entirely.
🎤
Feynman Technique
Deep Understanding
Explain a concept as if teaching a 10-year-old. Gaps in explanation reveal gaps in knowledge.
  • Pick a concept and write its name
  • Explain it simply, no jargon
  • Find where your explanation breaks down
  • Go back, study the gaps, repeat
Pro tip: Record yourself explaining it. Hearing yourself is different from writing — both are useful.
📝
Past Papers
1.5× EXP
The single best predictor of exam performance. Do under timed conditions always.
  • Find a paper from the last 3 years
  • Complete under strict timed conditions
  • Mark using the official mark scheme
  • Analyse every mark lost — add to your gap list
Pro tip: Don't just mark — write a one-sentence reason for every wrong answer.
